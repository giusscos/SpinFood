STEP-BY-STEP COOKING
Follow guided instructions with a clean, distraction-free view. Add a timer to any step — it runs as a Live Activity and alarms even when your phone is silent.

SMART SHOPPING LIST
Foo builds your shopping list from what you need. Check items off as you shop, then refill your pantry in one tap.

HOME SCREEN WIDGET & iCLOUD
Glance at pantry alerts and cooking suggestions from your Home Screen. Keep recipes, pantry, and history in sync across your devices with iCloud.

• Cooking stats — every meal charted over time
• Shopping lists — auto-generated and always ready
• Kitchen summary — recipes cooked, food consumed, pantry refills, and food waste insights

Available as a subscription or a one-time lifetime purchase.

*******************************

Foo has a warm, editorial aesthetic inspired by the cookbooks you love. Quiet, focused, and built around your kitchen — not algorithms.
