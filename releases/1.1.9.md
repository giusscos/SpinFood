# Foo — Version 1.1.9

Copy-ready App Store strings, organized by language. This release fixes getting stuck while writing recipe steps, adds a choice of how pages turn (fold, slide, or one at a time), turns the stats into a kitchen diary, and makes Foo work with VoiceOver, larger text, Increase Contrast, Reduce Motion and Differentiate Without Color.

**Store listing:** no change. Name, subtitle, keywords, screenshots and promotional text stay exactly as in 1.1.8, so the 1.1.8 before/after comparison keeps running. Only What's New is new.

## Why these changes

| What people met in 1.1.8 | What it did | 1.1.9 |
|---|---|---|
| In the page-curl book, a tap near either edge turned the page. "Index", "Done", "+" and "Steps" sit near the edges, so those taps often turned a page instead of pressing the button | People got stuck in the steps editor with no obvious way back, and left | Pages turn by swiping only; buttons always respond |
| Two page jumps could overlap, for example "Steps" pressed while a page was still turning | The book could show one page while it thought it was on another, and then "Steps" did nothing | Jumps wait their turn, and back always compares with the page on screen |
| The only way out of the steps editor was the index page | From step 5, two taps to get back to the recipe | The checkmark on every step returns to the recipe |
| Adding a step meant going back to the index each time | Slow to write a recipe | "Add Step N" at the end of the last step |
| The trash button deleted a step straight away | Easy to lose a step by mistake, now that it sits next to the checkmark | Asks "Delete Step N" first |
| Only the folding page turn | Too much motion for some people, and no option for Reduce Motion | Book, Slide or One at a Time, asked once at first start and changeable in Settings. One at a Time opens each page from the index with no swiping between pages; a swipe to the right goes back. Cooking still swipes from step to step |
| The recipe editor's time wheels loaded before the recipe's time and then wrote their default back | Saving any edit could change a recipe's time to 5 minutes | The wheels read and write the recipe's time directly |
| Step cards in the recipe editor were as tall as their text and centred | A short step looked smaller than the others | Equal height, aligned at the top |
| Dragging the amount ruler or a time wheel could also move the sheet or the page | A slightly diagonal drag closed the ingredient sheet or turned the page | Sideways drags on rulers, wheels and tag rows stay on the control; the ingredient and step-block sheets close with Cancel or Done |
| Pro was read only from StoreKit's "current entitlements" list, and replaced by whatever it returned | On the test phone that list came back empty while the subscription was active (trial started, group status "subscribed"), so the Pro pages stayed locked after buying | Also checks the subscription group status, Apple's recommended source, and unlocks straight from a successful purchase |
| On iOS 26.1+, locked Stats and Shopping showed "Upgrade to Pro" twice: on the page and above the tab bar | Looked broken | Only the button on the page |
| Stats were four big post-it totals | Numbers without a story, nothing about when or what | A Kitchen Diary: each month's ledger (cooked, eaten, saved, restocked, thrown away), the most cooked recipe, then day-by-day entries. The detailed charts are chapters at the end |
| Almost no accessibility support (a handful of VoiceOver labels) | VoiceOver read emoji names, "1h 30m" letter by letter, ★★★★ as "black star" four times, and every row as six separate pieces. Small labels ignored the text size setting | See below |

**Accessibility in 1.1.9**

- **VoiceOver:** each recipe, ingredient, inventory item, shopping item and step is one element with a clear name and details ("Garden Salad: 10 minutes, 2 servings, Easy, Vegan"). Times and ratings are spoken in words, headings are marked, decorative emoji and photos are skipped, and icon buttons have names ("Edit Recipe", "Add Ingredient", "Stop Timer"). Recipes have Edit and Delete actions, and the servings picker can be adjusted by swiping up or down. VoiceOver moves to the new page after every page turn, and reads out the "Saved from the bin" banner.
- **Larger Text:** small labels ("TONIGHT", "STEP", tags, section headers, titles) now grow with the text size. At the largest sizes, recipe rows and recipe details stack instead of being cut off.
- **Increase Contrast:** the orange gets darker, and cards, chips and tiles get an outline.
- **Reduce Motion:** no flying dishes or springs in onboarding, banners fade instead of sliding, and Slide mode fades between pages when you tap a button.
- **Differentiate Without Color:** missing ingredients get a symbol, not only the orange colour.
- **Selection state:** tags, Quick Add tiles, shopping items, checklist items and paywall plans are announced as selected.

## English (en-US)

Name, subtitle, keywords, promotional text: unchanged.

### What's New
```
• Going back while writing steps now always works, and you can return to the recipe from any step
• Add the next step straight from the last one, with a confirmation before a step is deleted
• Choose how pages turn: fold like a book, slide with less motion, or open one page at a time from the index. Change it anytime in Settings
• Better VoiceOver: recipes, ingredients, inventory and shopping items are read clearly, with headings and actions
• Text grows with your text size, and Foo adapts to Increase Contrast, Reduce Motion and Differentiate Without Color
```
