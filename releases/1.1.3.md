# What's New — 1.1.3

## English (en-US)

Home Screen Widget — glance at your pantry and cooking stats without opening the app.

iCloud Sync — your recipes, pantry, and history stay in sync across all your devices automatically.

Per-Step Cook Timers — add a timer to any recipe step. It runs on your Lock Screen as a Live Activity and rings an alarm even when your phone is on silent.

Barcode Scanner — scan a product barcode to add it to your pantry in seconds.

New onboarding — a fresh welcome experience that walks new users through everything Foo can do.

Plus improved recipe import with clearer error messages when something goes wrong.
