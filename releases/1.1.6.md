# Foo — Version 1.1.6

Copy-ready App Store strings, organized by language. This release is about conversion: a shorter onboarding, a rebuilt paywall and a product page that sells the free part of Foo first.

**Limits:** App Name ≤ 30 · Subtitle ≤ 30 · Keywords ≤ 100 bytes · Promotional text ≤ 170 · Description ≤ 4000 · Screenshot title ≤ 16 · Screenshot subtitle ≤ 32

---

## Why these changes (App Store Connect, last 90 days to Oct 14, 2026)

| Metric | Foo | Poly (for reference) |
|---|---|---|
| Impressions | 2.36K | 13K |
| Product page views | 111 (4.7% of impressions) | 605 (4.6%) |
| First-time downloads | 24 | 141 |
| Conversion rate (daily avg.) | 1.33% | 1.71% |
| In-app purchases | 0 | 6 |
| Day-7 download to paid | not enough data | 1.75% |

What that says:
- **Few people see Foo.** 2.36K impressions in 90 days is low, so search ranking is the biggest lever: the name and keywords below.
- **About 22% of page views download**, close to Poly. The screenshots and subtitle are worth sharpening, but they aren't broken.
- **Nobody paid.** In the app, the paywall sat behind an extra "See Plans" tap, the 3-recipe paywall never fired (it checked the onboarding flag), and on iOS 18 up to 26.0 the locked Summary and Shopping tabs had no upgrade button at all. All three are fixed in 1.1.6.

| Field | 1.1.5 | 1.1.6 | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| App Name | `Foo - Your Digital Recipe Book` | `Foo: Recipe Book & Pantry` | "Your" and "Digital" rank for nothing. "Pantry" is what makes Foo different from every other recipe app. |
| Subtitle | `Recipes, Pantry & Shopping` | `Cook what you have, waste less` | "Recipe" and "Pantry" move into the name, so the subtitle can carry the benefit plus new search words ("cook", "waste"). |
| Keywords | repeated `pantry`, `shopping` | no words from the name or subtitle | Apple already indexes name and subtitle words, so repeating them wastes bytes. |
| Promotional Text | features list | leads with the 7-day free trial | Promotional text can change without review. Switch back if the trial ends. |
| Description | "monthly or yearly" plans | weekly, yearly, lifetime, trial, what's free | The old text named plans that don't exist. App Review checks that the description matches the paywall. |

Foo is your personal kitchen companion: a beautiful recipe book that knows what's in your pantry, so you can cook what you already have and waste less food.

SMART SHOPPING LIST
Foo builds your shopping list from what your recipes need. Check items off as you shop, then refill your pantry in one tap.

READY TO COOK WIDGET & ICLOUD
See a recipe you can make right now on your Home Screen. Keep recipes, pantry and history in sync across your devices with iCloud.

FOO PRO
